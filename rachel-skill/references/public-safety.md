# Public Safety And Sharing

This skill is intended for legitimate, authorized digital-human production. It is not a consent bypass, identity impersonation tool, or hosted API service.

## Consent And Rights

Before cloning or generating:

- Confirm the user owns or has explicit permission to use the voice sample.
- Confirm the user owns or has explicit permission to use the portrait or avatar.
- Confirm the script and final use are allowed for the intended account, brand, client, or platform.
- Stop and ask for clarification if the request appears to impersonate a real person without consent.

## Disclosure

When publishing:

- Follow the target platform's AI-generated content disclosure requirements.
- Follow applicable local rules for synthetic media, advertising, endorsements, likeness rights, and biometric or voice data.
- Avoid presenting synthetic media as live, spontaneous, or personally recorded when that would mislead the audience.

Do not provide legal advice. If the user asks legal or regulatory questions, give a practical risk note and recommend checking the relevant platform policy or legal counsel.

## Public Sharing Checklist

When packaging or sharing this skill, include:

- `SKILL.md`
- `agents/openai.yaml`
- `references/`
- `scripts/`

Do not include:

- `.env` files
- API keys or bearer tokens
- real voice samples
- private portraits
- generated client videos
- real `job-state.json` files
- signed temporary download URLs
- local machine paths that reveal private folder names
